# 🔐 Security Audit – Botium Toys

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate** course, *Play It Safe: Manage Security Risks*.

The objective of this project was to conduct an **internal security audit** for a fictional company called **Botium Toys**. The audit focused on identifying security risks, evaluating existing controls, reviewing compliance requirements, and recommending improvements to strengthen the organization’s overall security posture.

I completed the portfolio activity with a **100% score**.

---

## 🏢 Scenario

Botium Toys is a small U.S.-based company that develops and sells toys through a physical store and an expanding online business.

As the company grows and begins serving customers in the United States and internationally, the IT department faces increased responsibility for protecting company systems, customer information, and business operations.

The IT manager requested an internal security audit to:

- Identify risks, threats, and vulnerabilities
- Review existing security controls
- Protect critical company assets
- Maintain business continuity
- Improve the organization's security posture
- Evaluate compliance requirements
- Reduce the possibility of financial or regulatory penalties

---

## 🎯 Audit Goals

The main goals of the audit were to:

- Review the company's current IT assets
- Analyze identified security risks
- Determine which security controls are implemented
- Identify missing or insufficient controls
- Evaluate compliance best practices
- Recommend improvements to reduce organizational risk

## 🛡️ Security Frameworks & Concepts

This project applied several cybersecurity frameworks and principles.

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework

The audit used concepts from the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF)** to evaluate the organization's security practices.

The framework focuses on the following functions:

- Govern
- Identify
- Protect
- Detect
- Respond
- Recover

---

### CIA Triad

The audit also considered the three main principles of the CIA Triad:

- **Confidentiality** – Protecting sensitive information from unauthorized access
- **Integrity** – Ensuring information remains accurate and unchanged
- **Availability** – Making sure systems and information remain accessible when needed

---

- Least privilege

## ⚠️ Threats, Risks, and Vulnerabilities

The audit evaluated the relationship between:

**Threats** – Events or actors that may cause harm

**Vulnerabilities** – Weaknesses that can potentially be exploited

**Risks** – The possible impact of a threat successfully exploiting a vulnerability

Understanding these differences helped determine which security controls should be prioritized.

## ⚖️ Compliance

Because Botium Toys processes online payments and serves international customers, the audit also considered regulatory and compliance requirements.

### PCI DSS

The **Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)** provides security requirements for organizations that process, store, or transmit payment card information.

Important practices include:

- Protecting cardholder data
- Restricting access to payment information
- Encrypting sensitive information
- Maintaining secure systems
- Monitoring access to payment systems

### GDPR

The **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** applies to organizations that process personal information belonging to individuals in the European Union.

Important practices include:

- Protecting personal data
- Limiting access to customer information
- Properly handling sensitive information
- Maintaining data privacy
- Reporting certain data breaches

## 💡 Security Recommendations

Based on the audit process, organizations in similar environments should consider implementing or strengthening:

- Least privilege access
- Separation of duties
- Strong password policies
- Multi-factor authentication
- Encryption of sensitive information
- Regular data backups
- Disaster recovery procedures
- Intrusion detection systems
- Centralized logging and monitoring
- Employee cybersecurity awareness training
- Regular vulnerability assessments
- Security audits and compliance reviews

These controls can help reduce risk and improve the organization's ability to protect sensitive systems and information.
